# Subterra

*[Leer en español](README.es.md)*

Subterra makes the underground of Kerbal Space Program's worlds reachable. Version 0.1.0 (alpha) adds lava tubes
to the Mun: open skylights in the maria lead down into tubes tens to hundreds of metres wide and kilometres long that
landers, rovers and kerbals on EVA can explore.

## What it does

- **Named sites**: Serenity Skylight (an easy first target near the equator), Great Gallery (a 500 m chamber),
  Kerman Chain (a 25 km tube with a row of skylights) and Frostvault (a dark polar tube with water ice).
- **Procedural networks** across the Mun's maria, the same in every game, found by exploring.
- **Real openings** in the terrain at the skylights, a debris rim around them, dark caps that show them from far away.
- **Caves you can use**: colliders around your vessels, landing and driving inside, quicksave/quickload, vessel
  switching and time warp work; the camera stays inside.
- **Environment**: darkness except under the skylights; parts take the rock's temperature (no freezing nights, no
  baking days).
- **Gameplay**: cave biomes for science (x1.5, named sites x2), richer Ore in the tubes (water ice with
  CommunityResourcePack), a message and waypoints when you discover a network, SCANsat anomalies for named sites.
- **Props**: breakdown boulders, rubble cones under the skylights, lavacicles, pahoehoe slabs, ice crystals.

## Requirements

- Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x
- ModuleManager
- Harmony (HarmonyKSP, `000_Harmony`)

Optional (detected automatically): SCANsat, Kerbalism, CommunityResourcePack, Parallax. Works with Kopernicus.

## Installation

Copy `GameData/Subterra` from the release zip into your KSP's `GameData` folder (CKAN: install "Subterra").

## Settings

`GameData/Subterra/Settings.cfg`: memory budget, ranges, waypoints, bodies or patches to turn off. Press
Alt+Shift+U in flight for the debug window (sites, the environment where the camera is, patch status).

## Known issues (alpha)

- Only the Mun has caves in this version.
- Cave walls use a box-projected rock texture; seams can show at sharp corners.
- The first visit to an area takes a few seconds to load the caves; vessels inside stay on rails until their
  colliders exist.

## Building

.NET SDK and a KSP 1.12.5 install for references: put its path in `Local.props` (`KSPRefDir`), then
`dotnet build Subterra.sln -c Release`. Core tests: `dotnet test tests/Subterra.Core.Tests -c Release`.
In-game scenarios: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/test-ksp.ps1 -Scenario smoke -Profile stock`
(runs a throwaway copy of KSP in `KSPTestDir`; never touches your game).
